# Notice Ledger design intent

Notice Ledger is an editorial public-record tool, not a trading dashboard. The visual language uses warm paper, ink navy, and one signal orange so evidence and outcome labels carry the hierarchy. Fraunces gives the product a record-room voice; DM Sans and DM Mono keep inputs, provenance, and transaction states legible.

The primary journey is one vertical evidence workflow: configure the deployed Studionet contract, create a provenance-bound draft, move it through real transactions, then inspect authoritative readback. Empty, pending, error, disconnected, and connected wallet states are explicit. No fake records, testimonials, balances, or decorative outcome claims are included.
